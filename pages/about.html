Our Story

Abigail's Collection is a feminine fashion brand based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Our brand was created around the idea that every woman deserves to express herself through unique and beautiful clothing.

We focus on creating distinctive dresses that celebrate femininity, creativity, and personal style.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a recognized fashion brand known for its unique designs, creativity, and dedication to women's fashion in Addis Ababa and beyond.

Our Values

  • Individuality: Celebrating every woman's personal style.
  • Creativity: Exploring unique and distinctive designs.
  • Elegance: Appreciating timeless and feminine fashion.
  • Quality: Valuing care and attention to detail.
